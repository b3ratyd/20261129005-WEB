CV

Hi, I'm Berat Yanardağ. I was born on 27 August 2007 in Sincan, Ankara. I completed primary, middle and high school successfully, then won a place at Akdeniz University and began studying Computer Programming. I'm currently a first-year student.

I work in software development, web interface design and digital visual production. I enjoy building projects that combine technical knowledge with visual detail: I build pages with HTML and CSS, bring them to life with JavaScript, and create my graphic work digitally. Findability (SEO) and accessibility are a natural part of the job too.

Alongside my studies I worked as a barista for eight months, where I learned to stay calm in a rush, communicate quickly and clearly with customers, and work as part of a team. Right now I'm focusing on website development and design, taking on new work to grow quickly.
